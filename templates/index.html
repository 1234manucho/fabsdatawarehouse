01 Hardware & identity

Integrate supported iris, fingerprint or face verification devices with enrollment, capture quality and device-supported liveness checks. The device and matching service are selected during discovery.

02 Permissions & access

Connect identity to staff roles, door access, attendance or application sign-in. A matched identity still needs permission for the requested action. Sensitive actions can require an additional approval.

03 Privacy & accountability

Plan enrollment consent, limited access to biometric templates, protected transport, retention controls and a non-biometric alternative. Keep a record of the decision without exposing raw biometric data.

Human-centred delivery
Can you integrate with our existing ERP or application?

We review your current system, available APIs and data structure before proposing an integration. The scope can cover payments, identity verification, reporting or a phased replacement of selected modules.

Does an iris match automatically grant access?

No. Identity verification and authorisation are separate steps. After a successful identity match, the connected system checks the person's role and the requested action. Access can still be denied when permission is missing.

What is needed for a live biometric integration?

A compatible capture device, an enrollment process, a matching service and a protected server integration. We agree supported devices, privacy controls, failure handling and an alternative access process during discovery.

How do you handle money in and money out?

Collection integrations associate provider-confirmed payments with invoices or orders. Disbursement integrations route approved instructions to the payout provider and return each payment's outcome for reconciliation. Supported rails and settlement terms are confirmed for the project.

Can an ERP be delivered in phases?

Yes. We can begin with priority departments or modules, validate them with users and expand in stages. The implementation plan defines dependencies, migration needs, training, acceptance criteria and the agreed delivery schedule.

What do we receive at handover?

The agreed solution, user training and documentation for operating it. Your scope should identify administrator guides, integration specifications, deployment instructions, support contacts and responsibility for backups and ongoing maintenance.
