Our Mission

One warehouse. Every workflow.

Businesses and governments in Africa have been forced to juggle a dozen disconnected tools — one for payments, another for payroll, a third for logistics, a fourth for compliance. We built Fabs Data Warehouse to end that fragmentation.

Our platform brings every financial and operational workflow under one authenticated session, one dashboard, and one auditable ledger — powered by M-Pesa, DPO, Pesapal, Finserve, Mifos and DHL.
